Water you can feel good about

Mindful showers, automatically.

Gotalia listens for your running shower and turns it into liters, time, and savings — without any hardware, smart meters, or setup. Everything happens on your phone.

How it works

No buttons to tap during your shower. Open it, set the phone down, and go.

Before

Start & set it down

Open Gotalia and place your phone near the shower. It starts listening locally for running water.

During

It tracks itself

Gotalia detects when the water starts, pauses, resumes, and stops — no interaction needed.

After

See your impact

Get a summary with duration, estimated liters, and how today compares with your usual shower.

What you get

Awareness, not restrictions. Gotalia makes invisible water use visible.

Automatic water detection

Detects running water using only your phone's microphone — no smart showerhead or meter.

Session tracking

Duration, active water time, and estimated consumption captured for every shower.

History

Look back over past showers and spot the patterns in your habits.

Insights dashboard

Weekly and monthly averages, water saved, and your progress over time.

Private by design

Gotalia analyses sound live, on your device to tell whether water is running. Your audio is never recorded, stored, or uploaded — ever.

The only data we ever collect is anonymous usage analytics to improve the app, and you can turn it off anytime. Read our privacy policy →
